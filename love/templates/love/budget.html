Brompton on Swale Bunkbarn

This is an accredited Bunkbarn inspected by Visit England. It is dog-friendly and has an area for securing bikes overnight. The village shop is directly opposite and has a large selection of food and drink, there is a pub 200 yards away and a larger pub 1/2 mile away. The barn has three bedrooms sleeping four in each one, a fully equipped kitchen, shower and a toilet. Duvets and pillows are provided, as well as sheet sleeping sacks and towels.
