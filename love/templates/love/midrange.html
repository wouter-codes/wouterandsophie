St Michael's Lodge

Experience comfort with character at St Michael's Lodge, nestled high above the River Swale in beautiful Swaledale. Hudswell Community Charity who now own the building transformed the church’s interior into a hostel with 17 beds across six bedrooms. The ground floor rooms are fully accessible, and the hostel features a resident’s lounge and a kitchen. The rooms mix historic charm with modern comfort. Each room accommodates 2 to 4 guests and offers the perfect balance of quality accommodation and value.

Amenities

  • Beds: 2, 3 or 4 beds per room
  • Bathroom: Private en-suite
  • Wi-Fi: Free Wi-Fi throughout the lodge
  • Facilities: Kitchen, drying room for wet gear, fire pit & outdoor seating, secure bike storage
  • Distance: 20 min drive from wedding

Why Choose This Option?

Get the benefits of private facilities while maintaining an affordable price point. This option is ideal for groups of 2-4 people. Each room has its own en-suite bathroom. The lodge is fully booked out for the wedding, so will only have other wedding guests.
